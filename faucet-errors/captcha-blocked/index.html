Faucet error: the captcha won't load or won't pass

What this error means, why the faucet returns it, and the fastest way to get your test tokens anyway.

Reviewed 30 August 2026.

The error

Faucets word this differently, but these are the same problem:

  • hCaptcha failed to load.
  • Please complete the captcha to continue.
  • Browser verification failed.

Why it happens

The faucet sits behind hCaptcha, reCAPTCHA, or Cloudflare browser verification. These are blocked or broken by a lot of ordinary setups: content blockers, strict tracking protection, VPN exit nodes with a bad reputation, and privacy browsers all trigger failures the faucet reports as a captcha error.

How to fix it

  1. Disable your content blocker for the faucet domain. This fixes the majority of "captcha failed to load" cases outright.
  2. Turn the VPN off for the claim. Datacenter and shared-exit IPs are scored as high-risk, and captcha providers fail them regardless of what you do in the widget.
  3. Try a mainstream browser profile with default settings. Strict anti-fingerprinting settings break hCaptcha's challenge flow.
  4. Pick a faucet that doesn't use one. Our per-token pages flag captcha-gated faucets, and the homepage has a Hide captcha-gated filter.
